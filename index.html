Quick guide & precision

Try sqrt(81), sin(30), or 2^5. Adjacent values multiply: 2π and 2(3+4).

DEG uses degrees; RAD uses radians. Inverse trig returns the selected unit. History restores the original angle mode.

% divides by 100: 200×10% = 20. Ans inserts the previous answer.

Powers associate right: 2^3^2 = 512. -2^2 = -4; (-2)^2 = 4. Multiplication and division, including implicit multiplication, evaluate left to right. Use parentheses to avoid ambiguity.

Standard floating-point arithmetic, with 12 significant digits displayed. Internal answers retain available precision. Rounding errors, overflow, and underflow are possible.
